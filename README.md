Interactive Power BI dashboard focused on animal adoption trends, shelter performance and adoption drivers.

This project explores animal outcomes, adoption behavior and shelter efficiency through an interactive multi-page dashboard built with **Power BI**, **DAX** and **Python** for data preparation and analysis.


---

## 📊 Project Overview

The dashboard was designed to analyze:

* Total animals and adoptions
* Adoption rate
* Shelter stay duration
* Adoption trends over time
* Animal type distribution
* Adoption patterns by sex and sterilization status
* Business insights derived from the analysis

The project combines storytelling, interactive navigation and business-oriented insights to create a complete analytical dashboard experience.

## 📌 Overview

General overview of:

* Total animals
* Total adoptions
* Adoption percentage
* Animal outcomes
* Monthly adoption trends
* Animal type distribution

### Main Findings

* Dogs represent the majority of animals and adoptions.
* Adoption is the most common outcome overall.
* Adoption activity changes during different periods of the year.

---

## 📈 Adoption Drivers Analysis

Focused analysis on the main factors influencing adoptions.

### Includes:

* Average shelter stay
* Average adoption time
* Share of adoptions by animal type
* Adoption patterns by sex and sterilization status

### Main Findings

* Adopted animals usually find a home within approximately 23 days.
* Some animals remain in shelters significantly longer than the average adoption time.
* Dogs stay longer in shelters compared to cats.
* Intact animals are adopted more frequently than sterilized animals.

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Business Findings

Final analytical summary of the project.

### Key Insights

* Dogs account for around 66% of total adoptions.
* Shelter efficiency appears generally positive, although some animals remain significantly longer in shelters.
* Adoption activity shows seasonal variations during the year.
* Sterilization status may influence adoption patterns.
